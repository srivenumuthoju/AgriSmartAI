🚜 Basic Farming Recommendations & Best Practices

  • Sowing & Land Prep: Level puddled clayey loam soil thoroughly. Transplant 20-25 day old seedlings.
  • Irrigation Management: Maintain shallow standing water (2-5 cm) during vegetative stage.
  • Fertilizer Schedule: Apply Nitrogen in three split doses (basal, tillering, panicle initiation).
  • Pest & Disease Control: Monitor for stem borer and bacterial blight; use bio-pesticides.
